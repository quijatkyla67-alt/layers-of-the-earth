⭐ IM 2 — Life of a Star

Stars form in clouds of gas and dust called nebulae. Their life paths depend strongly on their mass.

1. Nebula

Gravity pulls gas and dust together.

2. Main Sequence

A stable stage where hydrogen fusion powers the star.

3. Giant Stage

The star expands as its fuel supply changes.

4. Final Stage

Lower-mass stars end as white dwarfs; very massive stars can explode as supernovae and leave neutron stars or black holes.

Challenge: Why does a massive star usually have a shorter life than a lower-mass star?

Hint: Think about how quickly it uses its nuclear fuel.
